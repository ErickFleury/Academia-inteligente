## 3. Input contract

Consume the authenticated student's **completed and valid** structured onboarding
through the existing shared validation/service boundary:

| Fact | Actual representation | Meaning/limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Goal | `training_goal`, free text | Preserve actual text; confirm ambiguous/mixed goals before mapping. |
| Experience | `none`, `beginner`, `intermediate`, `advanced` | Self-report; no automatic equivalence to a study's training-status categories. |
| Body measures | `height_cm`, `weight_kg` | Existing units; neither a strength baseline nor an age/health diagnosis. |
| Limitations/complaints | Boolean plus conditional detail | User report; no diagnosis inferred from a painful region. |
| Medications | Boolean plus conditional detail | Consider only relevant, supported interactions; no drug identification/dose/effect invented from incomplete text. |
| Conditions | Boolean plus conditional detail | Reported context; not blanket exercise clearance or exclusion. |

Inputs are minimized to the relevant policy and remain own-client/functional-need
only. External literature-discovery services receive public literature queries,
never real onboarding or raw chat. Authorized internal retrieval/AI context keeps
the existing minimized own-client boundary and never populates shared evidence
with client facts. Administrative identifiers do not enter AI context or the shared corpus.
Completed onboarding is not rewritten from model extraction or later messages.

Missing age/population eligibility, weekly schedule, baseline capacity or required
clearance cannot be inferred. Use the authorized clarification path when its
source/confirmation is permitted; otherwise abstain for the dependent request.
New medical collection/retention needs its existing DEC-18 decision. Equipment
and exact saved plan revisions come from their existing services, not onboarding.
Per-fact authority, confirmation, observation/time validity and conflict behavior
remain required in the versioned snapshot; no universal health-data expiry.

## 4. Exercise identity contract

R1 is the selected professional starting reference for a bounded reviewed
dictionary. Each entry requires a stable `exercise_key`, original pt-BR label,
reviewed aliases/variations, movement, primary and secondary muscle relationships,
equipment constraints, source locator, attribution/reuse status, dictionary version
and review validity. Do not equate a body-region label with a precise muscle
contribution or copy ACE descriptions/media into local records without permission.
General anatomy can use the separately licensed existing Biga seed within its terms.

Unknown/ambiguous identity rejects qualification. Similar spelling is insufficient
to merge exercises; machine/free-weight/assisted variants need explicit mappings.
Technique descriptions cannot establish numerical dosing or prove suitability for
a student's complaint. Specific restriction adjustments require applicable evidence.
No exercise CRUD or media import is added by this spec.

## 5. Prescription and instructor contracts

Use R2 as the professional framework candidate, R3 for its actual healthy-adult
strength/hypertrophy scope, and appropriate condition-specific guidance when
onboarding requires it. These sources guide **research and rule review**; excluded
full text must not enter embeddings/writer context or act as runtime support.
Missing reusable applicable evidence remains a gap until permission or an adequate
eligible source is verified. Do not substitute a weaker licensed source silently.

Every material rule must record: actual goal/population/restriction applicability;
outcome-specific finding/certainty and limitations; benefits, harms and feasibility;
source version/hash/passage/locator; direct finding versus professional derivation;
typed choices/bounds/units; required inputs; conditions and conflict resolution;
reviewer identities, dates/deadlines and policy version. Reuse existing appraisals
with an explicit adopt/adapt/reassess record rather than inventing new ratings.

Mandatory dimensions: exercise selection, session/week volume where claimed,
intensity, frequency/recovery, progression/regression, rest and restriction
adjustments. A general hypertrophy reference plus an unrelated clinical citation
does not establish support for the combined prescription. Medication use or an
injury alone cannot be an automatic rejection; an unresolved relevant interaction
or missing mandatory input/support prevents this candidate from qualifying.

No global sets/repetitions/rest/weekly-volume optimum is specified here. Concrete
parameters must come from appraised applicable findings and documented professional
choices. Percent-1RM/absolute loads need their required measured inputs. Progression
is conditional guidance with an authorized observable trigger, not automatic plan
mutation. Displayed values derive from the validated typed contract; extra writer
prose cannot add a competing dose or unsupported diagnosis.

The instructor reviews the exact saved revision and may approve/activate only
through existing instructor authorization. Approval is mandatory and distinct
from the bibliographic/prescription checks. It cannot rescue a discarded AI
candidate or create missing evidence. An instructor may author/edit or explicitly
assume an already saved accessible draft manually under the separate audited
workflow; this clears current AI qualification and preserves lineage/history.
No automated activation or silent historical rewrite is authorized.

The owner's subsequent all-goal research request is detailed in the
[rule review package](training-prescription-rule-review.md) and its
[structured rule registry](training-prescription-rule-candidates.json).
It covers strength, hypertrophy, weight/fat loss, cardiorespiratory fitness,
local muscular endurance, power, mobility/flexibility, balance, functional autonomy,
general health and sport performance. These are research families, not an enum
that replaces free-text goals. Each profile references every mandatory dimension
and accounting, but unresolved decisions remain explicit and block enablement.
Actual source numbers, historical recommendations, professional published
examples and proposed translations are distinguished; none is fabricated from
the synthetic test fixtures or represented as a completed professional review.
An unspecified sport or a static-flexibility source cannot establish a complete
sport-specific or active-mobility prescription. See the review package for source
access, conflicts, input dependencies and promotion into executable policies.

### 5.1. Weight loss / fat loss — research direction, awaiting policy review

The owner explicitly requests exercise guidance for students seeking weight loss.
Keep the original goal: weight reduction, fat reduction and muscle preservation
are different outcomes. Clarify combined priorities, baseline activity, available
schedule, preferences and applicable health facts through authorized inputs.
The request alone establishes neither overweight/obesity nor a safe weight target.

Findings selected for review:

| Reference | Finding and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| R7, public abstract | Progress activity appropriately toward at least **150 minutes/week of moderate-intensity activity**; weight-management effects show a dose-response. This is not a mandatory starting workload, guaranteed result or universal optimum. HIIT was not superior for weight regulation; no single activity mode was established as superior, and multimodal activity is encouraged for broader benefits. Appetite/energy-intake responses vary. Medical-treatment contexts require medical appropriateness rather than a blanket clearance. |
| R6, WHO fact sheet | For adult health, **150–300 minutes/week moderate aerobic activity**, or **75–150 vigorous**, or an equivalent combination; major-muscle strengthening on **at least two days/week**. These are health recommendations, not a guaranteed weight-loss dose. Adult figures cannot simply be assigned to children or every life-stage/condition combination. |
| R8, publisher-indexed findings | During dietary weight loss in people living with overweight/obesity, adding resistance exercise helped preserve fat-free mass and increased fat-mass loss without a further difference in body-mass change. Fat-free mass is not identical to muscle mass. Heterogeneity and prediction intervals limit confidence in individual results; no universal resistance dose or individual effect size is derived here. |

Proposed application decisions, subject to the existing professional review:

1. Seek an individually applicable combination of aerobic and resistance exercise;
   do not replace resistance work with cardio solely because the goal is weight
   loss. The exact balance, starting dose, intensity method, progression, recovery
   and restriction adjustments still need complete reviewed rules. Combining R3
   and R7 does not itself validate a simultaneous hypertrophy/weight-loss program.
2. Do not promise kilograms lost, a deadline, localized fat reduction or an exact
   calorie expenditure. Do not convert exercise minutes/tonnage to kilograms of
   fat or use a generic calories-per-kilogram calculation as a validated result.
   Dietary intake affects outcomes; no meal plan, calorie-deficit target,
   supplement or medication prescription is added to this training scope.
3. Count aerobic **duration, intensity category and actual weekly occurrences**
   separately from resistance sets/repetitions/load. Do not call all gym time
   aerobic time or count concurrent minutes twice. An unknown intensity remains
   unknown; moderate/vigorous equivalence needs an approved explicit method.
4. Preserve system access for students reporting medications, complaints or
   injuries. Relevant interactions and combined-goal prescriptions need applicable
   evidence; do not silently assign vigorous activity or clear a medical context.
5. Include reviewed positive and rejection cases for weight-loss-only and mixed
   goals, missing activity/schedule facts, wrong-population citations, unsupported
   dose conversions and medication/complaint contexts. These are evaluation
   requirements, not completed tests or a sealed independent case set.

Coverage status is **partial / awaiting professional review**: references exist;
complete numeric policies, reusable supporting passages, aerobic typed-contract
coverage and operational evaluation remain pending. The isolated resistance
contract must not encode cardio minutes as repetitions/rest or claim this goal
already works end to end. No JSON policy, model prompt, runtime corpus or release
flag is changed by this research addition. No new measurements/UI are implemented.

## 6. Accounting contract — proposed method v1

R4 motivates distinguishing direct from indirect work. R5 supplies context for
composite dose measures. The following are **engineering/accounting definitions
proposed for professional review**, not physiological thresholds or approved
fractional muscle allocations:

| Quantity | Computation and prerequisites |
| --- | --- |
| Exercise/session sets | Sum each item's explicitly prescribed working sets once for the actual session occurrence. Separate warm-up entries; an unclassified set cannot silently become a working set. |
| Seven-day exercise sets | Sum actual scheduled session occurrences in the declared seven-day window. An A/B/C label or a saved sheet alone supplies no frequency. |
| Direct muscle sets | Sum working sets whose reviewed dictionary/accounting mapping classifies the muscle as directly targeted. |
| Indirect muscle sets | Track separately where that mapping classifies a secondary contribution. Do not add as full direct sets or silently discard from interpretation. |
| Fractional estimate | Only when an applicable reviewed policy supplies weights: `direct_sets + sum(indirect_sets * approved_weight)`, with method/version and uncertainty. R4's 0.5 model is a research candidate, not a default for every muscle/exercise. |
| Repetition count | Sum `sets * repetitions` only when repetitions are explicit and compatible. Retain ranges as ranges and duration-based items separately. |
| External-load volume | Sum actual `repetitions * external_load_kg` per set only with valid values and consistent load conventions; never infer bodyweight contribution, unilateral multiplier or machine equivalence. |
| Frequency/recovery | Count actual planned occurrences and intervals independently of inter-set `rest_seconds`; apply the policy's scope and recovery checks. |

Muscle involvement across a multi-joint exercise need not sum to one; never
invent fractional contributions to force a partition. Do not compare raw load
totals across non-equivalent equipment or confuse tonnage with hypertrophy dose.
Record unilateral/per-side conventions and exercise variation before aggregation.
If classification, schedule or load conventions are unknown, return the affected
quantity as unknown and reject any qualification that depends on it. Unknown is
not zero. A session-only prescription can qualify only its declared scope when
its applicable policy does not require unavailable weekly context.

Worked **synthetic accounting example**, not training advice: a scheduled session
with two working sets occurs twice in a week. Exercise total is four sets. If an
approved mapping classifies a muscle as directly involved, direct count is four;
if indirectly involved, indirect count is four in its separate column. No weighted
total exists until an approved applicable weighting method is selected.

## 7. Evaluation and rejection contract

The owner's minimum rule is mandatory: **reject a prescription with material gaps
or no sources**. Completion requires more than a nonempty references list:

1. Each mandatory dimension/material decision resolves to an applicable approved
   policy rule and a current, allowed, actually retrieved passage/version/locator.
2. References must support the actual claim, goal, input population and restriction
   combination. Fabricated/unretrieved IDs, unrelated anatomy citations, expired
   reviews or a writer's own `supported` label cannot authorize a plan.
3. Typed units, exercise identities, doses, aggregates, inputs, concurrency and
   revision/evidence integrity must pass. Numerical completeness without support
   is still a failure; sources without required parameters are also insufficient.
4. Rejection discards the attempt without publishing its table, actionable prose,
   partial operations or review action. Preserve the saved draft/current/history.
5. Distinguish missing input, insufficient support, documented restriction conflict
   and technical outage. Lack of evidence does not prove harm or prohibit exercise.
   Ask a permitted focused question, retry a technical failure within budget, or
   offer the existing instructor/manual path, as appropriate.

Evaluation must include supported positive controls as well as rejection cases;
an always-reject implementation fails. Development tests cover references that
exist but do not support dose, missing progression/rest, wrong population,
unresolved combined restrictions, medication report without an applicable rule,
unknown goals/inputs, source revocation, outages and stale revisions. Include
medicated/injured/complaint-positive cases **when an applicable reviewed rule and
reference outcome exist**; disclosure alone is never the negative oracle.

Use Task 45's prespecified evaluation design and Task 47's required independent
seal before independent evaluation and operational enablement. The
[approved development exception](training-prescription-development-amendment.md)
permits isolated Tasks 48–50 development beforehand, without real-student release.
Held-out answers remain outside developer
fixtures/prompts/runtime evidence. Report support/unsupported acceptance, false
rejection and reason accuracy with cohort/attempt denominators and uncertainty.
Zero critical failures and passing positive controls are existing product gates,
not proof of zero real-world risk. Actual-deployment repeated evaluation and named
scoped release approval remain mandatory; no new arbitrary success percentage.

## 8. Audience, acceptance and implementation handoff

All students with valid authorized access can use the system and request adapted
training, with or without medications, injuries, complaints or reported conditions.
The same process evaluates each goal and relevant health context. Requests needing
more evidence receive an explicit result and professional path; no blanket
`has_health_conditions => reject` or `uses_medications => reject` rule is permitted.
Age/life-stage requirements still need actual facts and applicable evidence; the
WHO inclusion reference does not fill missing individual data or clear all groups.

Example required behavior: a hypertrophy request from a student reporting a knee
complaint preserves that goal, uses their own onboarding, identifies the relevant
unknowns without diagnosing, and seeks support for both hypertrophy and the
actual adjustment. A fully supported candidate may become an unapproved proposal
for the instructor. Otherwise the attempt is rejected/clarified with no fabricated
workout; the student retains system access and instructor support.
