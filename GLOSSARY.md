# Academia Inteligente

Terms for the gym's client-facing training assistance and its supporting knowledge.

## Language

**Client training assistant**:
The client-facing assistant that supports conversational onboarding, initial
training-plan proposals, training questions, and adaptation proposals. It does
not replace the instructor responsible for reviewing and approving plans.
_Avoid_: Autonomous instructor

**Training reference**:
Training guidance, exercise instructions, or equipment documentation supplied
by an administrator from a source deemed trustworthy. A training reference is
distinct from an individual client's reported information or approved plan.
_Avoid_: Client memory, client record

**Client memory**:
A compact, evolving collection of attributed and dated client-reported health
information, preferences, and training goals, retained across conversations
until individually forgotten or the Client is deleted. It is distinct from
authoritative onboarding and approved training plans; retained reports are not
necessarily current facts.
_Avoid_: Chat transcript, medical record
